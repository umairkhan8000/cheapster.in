Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Cheapster.in. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use our website and participate in our giveaways.

2. Information We Collect

When you use the "Login with Google" feature on our website, we securely request and collect the following information provided by your Google account:

  • Name: To identify you as a participant in our giveaways.
  • Email Address: To verify your account, prevent duplicate or fraudulent giveaway entries, and maintain platform security.
  • Profile Picture (Optional): Displayed locally on your screen to confirm you are logged in.

We do not collect banking passwords, UPI PINs, OTPs, or any sensitive financial data. Our directory consists of outbound affiliate links; any purchases made are handled entirely by the respective third-party brands on their secure domains.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used strictly for the following purposes:

  • To authenticate your identity via Firebase Auth.
  • To register your entry into the Cheapster.in Festive Giveaway.
  • To contact you securely via WhatsApp (if you voluntarily provide your phone number in the giveaway form) in the event you are selected as a winner.

4. Data Sharing and Protection

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase databases. We only publicly announce the first name and reward amount of our giveaway winners on our official social media channels to maintain transparency.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions or comments about this policy or wish to request the deletion of your data, you may email us at: support@cheapster.in.
